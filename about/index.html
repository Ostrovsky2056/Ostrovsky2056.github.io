How screenshots stay on-device

Screenshot Redactor processes images, recognized text, and masks in browser memory. The browser briefly holds files selected on the homepage and clears the temporary copy when the editor reads it. The first scan downloads models and WebAssembly files. Your screenshots and recognition results stay on your device.
